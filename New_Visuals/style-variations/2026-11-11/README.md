## Shared prompt

> Generate an image. A 16:9 mock gameplay screenshot of a top-down 2D space game, strict overhead orthographic view (no perspective, no horizon, no UI, no text, no logos). In the center is the player's ship, built from about 14 square modules joined edge-to-edge on a grid: a core block in the middle, several gun turret modules, a missile pod, a shield generator, a reactor and armor plates. It must clearly read as separate square tiles forming one irregular ship. Around it an enemy swarm arrives in grouped formations: a cluster of about 20 tiny drones, a wedge of 6 fighters, 4 heavy armored brutes, and one large boss ship about five times bigger than a module. A few projectiles and small explosions. Deep space background. The player ship is cool white and teal, enemies are warm red and orange, so the two sides read at a glance. ART STYLE - *(one of the lines below)*. The ship, the enemies, the boss and the background must all share exactly this one art style.

## Style lines

- **A:** clean ceramic industrial: smooth white ceramic armor panels on graphite mounts, flat soft shading, large simple readable shapes, thin teal light accents, enemies built in the same manufacturing language in rust-orange and graphite.
- **B:** heavy industrial salvage: thick weathered steel plates, bolts and rivets, welded seams, hazard stripes, soot and scratches, chunky practical machinery, muted gunmetal and off-white player ship with teal signal lights; enemies are scrap-built raiders in oxidized red and burnt orange. Gritty but with large readable shapes.
- **C:** inked comic cel: bold black ink outlines of varying weight, flat cel colours with one hard shadow tone, small halftone dot shading, slightly chunky exaggerated proportions, like a hand-drawn sci-fi comic seen from above.
- **D:** chunky pixel art: crisp low-resolution pixel art with a limited palette, each ship module about 32 by 32 pixels, hard pixel edges, no blur, no gradients, simple two-tone shading, like a modern indie factory and tower-defense pixel game seen from above.
- **E:** dark painterly naval sci-fi: hand-painted gunmetal and deep navy hulls with soft brushwork, glowing teal energy lines on the player ship, glowing ember-orange lights on enemy ships, subtle nebula, moody and cinematic but with clear silhouettes.

## Status

All five were rejected on 2026-11-11. The existing style stays, and the game should not move toward realism. These files are kept as a record only.
